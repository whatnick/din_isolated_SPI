# DIN isolated SPI carrier v2.0

Open `din_isolated_SPI.kicad_pro`; its matching modern schematic and PCB are
the canonical design. The original schematic and project are retained in
`legacy\` for reference, not as the active design.

The PCB envelope is **31.75 × 40.513 mm**, X 116.84–148.59 and
Y 50.927–91.44 mm, with four R2 mm outer corners. The original 0.635 mm
chamfers were replaced without moving connector or component datums. The
rectangular isolation slot remains at X 127–138.43, Y 77.47–79.502 mm:
all four physical edges are retained. The redundant zero-length edge at
(127, 77.47) was removed. `mechanical\din_isolated_SPI-v2-board.step` is a
board-only export; its measured PCB thickness is 1.51 mm.

Compared with the R2 source, pad geometry, component placement, and pad-net
membership are unchanged. Only two 0.127 mm dangling copper stubs were
removed (R2.1/U1.3 and R3.1/U1.4); no other route geometry or net assignment
changed. Silkscreen and reference positions were corrected, with obscured
host-outline graphics retained on Fab. Autogenerated net names and legacy
symbol identifiers were modernized without changing the circuits.

J1 pins 1–10 are `3V3_C`, `GND_C`, `CS2_E`, `CS0_E`, `CLK_E`, `MISO_E`,
`MOSI_E`, `CS1_E`, `GND_I`, `3V3_I`. The host-side and isolated-side supplies
and grounds remain separate. U4's unused pins 1 and 5–11 are explicitly
unconnected. Host compatibility and signal direction must be checked against
the actual module, not inferred from ERC or the historical WeMos symbol.

## Validation

With KiCad 10.0 installed, run:

```powershell
python tools\validate_carrier.py
```

The script runs KiCad ERC and refilled PCB DRC with schematic parity,
`--severity-all` and `--exit-code-violations`; checks the source R2 pad,
net, track and outline snapshot; verifies model paths; and records SHA-256
hashes of the persisted LF-normalized project and reports in
`validation\manifest.json`. The R2 input SHA-256 was
`c6f831b37b46a6d9e7e5a380abb491db80ff253bbde58dba4960276e59631a85`.

The original router files `din_isolated_SPI.dsn` and
`din_isolated_SPI.rules` specify 0.15 mm Default clearance and 0.5 mm Power
clearance. The modern project preserves these and the original Power nets;
it has no DRC exclusions. The reports include errors, warnings and exclusions.
KiCad's unchanged default ignored checks are listed in the JSON reports
(four ERC checks and five DRC checks); zero findings does not mean those
disabled checks were evaluated.

## Mechanical and sourcing limits

The assembly's short-header **2.5 mm envelope is an external mechanical
assumption**, not a verified model in this project. The installed socket
models are the existing tracked models, and the historical
`wemos_d1_mini_pro_board.step` is empty: no module model or S3 electrical or
mechanical compatibility is established. Check enclosure clearance, actual
header engagement and strain physically.

Existing model provenance links are in `3dmodels\attribution.md`; their
redistribution licences and accuracy have not been independently established.
No new third-party CAD was downloaded. The inherited R1 schematic value is
50 Ω while its `CRCW060347R0JNEB` sourcing field suggests 47 Ω; verify the
intended fitted part before ordering. ERC/DRC and a STEP export are not
isolation-safety, manufacturing, or enclosure-fit approval.
